可加入員工範圍 / Eligible Employees

凡受僱於台灣美光晶圓科技股份有限公司、台灣美光記憶體股份有限公司及美商美光亞太科技股份有限公司（包含 Micron Technology, Inc. 在台灣投資的相關公司），年滿 16 歲之員工，除代表雇方行使管理權之主管人員及人事主管人員（經理監事決議者不限）外，均依本會章程辦理入會。

Employees aged 16 or older who are employed by Taiwan Micron Technology, Inc., Micron Memory Taiwan Co., Ltd., Micron Asia Pacific entities, or other Micron Technology, Inc. invested companies in Taiwan may apply for membership in accordance with the Union Bylaws, except personnel who exercise management authority on behalf of the employer or serve in human-resources management roles, unless otherwise approved by resolution of the Union Board and Supervisor.

相關法規說明 / Relevant Legal Framework

本會之組織、運作、會員權利義務、勞資關係及相關勞動權益事項，均依中華民國現行相關法令辦理，包括但不限於《勞動基準法》、《工會法》、《團體協約法》、《勞資爭議處理法》、《性別平等工作法》及《電子簽章法》等相關規定。

本會將依相關法令及工會章程推動會務、保障會員合法勞動權益，並促進勞資雙方之溝通與合作。

The organization and operation of the Union, the rights and obligations of its members, labor-management relations, and other matters concerning labor rights shall be handled in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations of the Republic of China (Taiwan), including but not limited to the Labor Standards Act, Labor Union Act, Collective Agreement Act, Act for Settlement of Labor-Management Disputes, Act of Gender Equality in Employment, Electronic Signatures Act, and other relevant laws and regulations.

The Union shall conduct its affairs in accordance with applicable laws and the Union Bylaws, safeguard the lawful labor rights and interests of its members, and promote constructive communication and cooperation between labor and management.
入會與會費說明 / Membership & Union Fee Information

入會費：依工會規定及通知繳納。
Registration Fee: Payable in accordance with Union rules and notice.

每月經常會費：新臺幣 50 元。會費委由公司代扣之方案，以及經常會費與團結基金等月費之繳納方式，依理監事會議決議辦理。
Monthly Regular Union Dues: NT$50 per month. Payroll deduction arrangements and the payment method for monthly dues, including regular dues and any solidarity fund, are handled in accordance with resolutions of the Union Board and Supervisor.

入會申請書 / Membership Application
本人為台灣美光關係企業工會會員，同意依本會章程及相關法令，自本人提出申請後，於應繳經常會費當月，由本人所屬公司自本人每月薪資中代扣本會每月經常會費新臺幣 50 元，並將扣取之會費轉交工會。會費委由公司代扣之方案，以及經常會費與團結基金等月費之繳納方式，依理監事會議決議辦理。

如本人當月薪資不足扣款，則依工會其他繳費方式辦理。如本人欲改以其他方式繳納經常會費，將自行向工會提出取消薪資代扣會費之申請，再由公司停止自本人薪資中扣取工會經常會費。

English
I am a member of the Taiwan Taiwan Micron Enterprise Affiliate Union. In accordance with the Union Bylaws and applicable laws, I authorize my employing Micron company, after this application is submitted, to deduct the monthly regular Union dues of NT$50 from my salary in the month in which the dues are payable and remit the deducted dues to the Union. Payroll deduction arrangements and the payment method for monthly dues, including regular dues and any solidarity fund, are handled in accordance with resolutions of the Union Board and Supervisor.

If my salary for a given month is insufficient for the deduction, I will pay the dues using another payment method provided by the Union. If I wish to change to another method of paying the monthly Union dues, I will submit a request to the Union to cancel the payroll deduction authorization, after which the Company will stop deducting the monthly Union dues from my salary.
